GenuineReviewsOnly

Open any product page and see only the reviews that look genuine. Fake, paid and "free product in exchange for a review" posts are hidden automatically.

1 · Install the browser extension (works on any link)

  1. Download the extension and unzip it.
  2. Chrome / Edge / Brave: open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder.
  3. Firefox: open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox, click Load Temporary Add-on and pick extension/manifest.json.
  4. Open any product link (Amazon, or any shop that marks up its reviews) and scroll to the reviews. A green badge in the bottom-right shows how many were hidden. Click Show hidden to see what was removed and why.

The extension runs entirely in your browser. It does not send your browsing or the reviews anywhere.

2 · Or paste reviews here to check them

Copy reviews from any site and paste them below. Put a blank line between reviews. You can start a review with a rating like 5/5 or ★★★★★, and add [verified] or [unverified] if the site shows it.

How it decides

Each review gets a suspicion score from signals common in fake reviews: incentive disclosures, unverified purchases, very short 5-star or 1-star posts, generic praise phrases, piles of superlatives and exclamation marks, links, near-identical copies of other reviews, and same-day bursts of 5-star reviews. Concrete usage details (time used, measurements, comparisons, downsides) lower the score.

This is a heuristic filter, not proof. It will occasionally hide a real review or miss a clever fake. Use "Show hidden" to check.
